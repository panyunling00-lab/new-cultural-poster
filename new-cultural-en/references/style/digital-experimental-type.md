# Digital Experimental Type

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Use modular computational graphic transformations as the visual subject with restrained experimental glyph character in event copy.

## Typography

Design letterforms with theme-led scale, distortion, layering and unequal rhythm. Use typography-moves.md as optional inspiration. Preserve all required characters and normal-size readability; large or interleaved text is allowed.

## Palette

Favor high-contrast digital graphic palettes: black, white, off-white, charcoal, electric green, cobalt blue, vivid orange, and signal red. Use monochromatic combinations for structural experiments or introduce one or two saturated accents to emphasize modular relationships and visual operations. Prioritize clear, flat colors and controlled contrast over decorative gradients.

## Imagery

Use theme-related modular geometry, segmentation, raster structure and rule-based transformation as the primary visual. Do not use giant event-letter matrices or invented pseudo-text.

## Texture / Material

Crisp vector edges, rasterized type, pixel structures, scanline patterns, fine linear textures, digital halftones, optical interference, and controlled print grain. Combine precise geometric construction with selective raster degradation or digitally simulated distortion. Keep material effects subordinate to the typographic transformation rules rather than relying on decorative technology textures.

## Composition Rules

- Lettering can be the principal visual object.
- Use the selected deformation/material mechanism on letterforms, not just background decoration.
- Allow full-bleed overlay, uneven placement and legible occlusion.
- Functional copy remains accessible; no size, area or negative-space caps.

## Avoid

Generic futuristic fonts, decorative cyberpunk effects, meaningless glitch noise, random character scattering, arbitrary glyph distortion, excessive neon gradients, fake coding interfaces, unnecessary 3D typography, unrelated geometric clutter, uncontrolled algorithmic patterns, illegible essential information, and digital effects without a coherent underlying transformation rule.
