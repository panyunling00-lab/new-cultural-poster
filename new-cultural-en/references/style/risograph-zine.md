# Risograph Zine

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Create a vibrant independent-publishing poster with limited ink colors, grainy overprint, handmade imperfection, and zine energy.

## Typography

- Use friendly bold sans-serif, hand-rendered display type, or typewriter/mono accents.
- Keep copy short and punchy.
- Allow slight misregistration or handmade unevenness.

## Palette

Limited riso inks: fluorescent pink, orange, soy blue, teal, sunflower yellow, purple, black, cream paper.

## Imagery

Simple illustrations, doodles, icons, cutouts, hand marks, pattern blocks, rough shapes.

## Texture / Material

Risograph grain, overprint, misregistration, paper fibers, ink speckle, scan texture.

## Composition Rules

- Use 2-3 ink colors maximum.
- Embrace print imperfection but keep hierarchy readable.
- Use playful blocks and repeated motifs.
- Make it feel locally printed and tactile.

## Avoid

Polished corporate gradients, too many colors, unreadable handwriting, messy doodle overload, and glossy 3D effects.
