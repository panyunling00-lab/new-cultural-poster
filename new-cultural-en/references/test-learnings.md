### Previously recorded successes

- Landscape 16:9 overlay completed end to end in one task. Reported title height 11.4% (target ≤15%, cap 20%), text area 4.4% (cap 20%), and exact official designations. Explicit canvas landscape 16:9 triggered landscape prompt instructions.
- is_landscape reads width:height and landscape/horizontal/the source's Chinese landscape word. Landscape briefs receive long-axis/side-information instructions; portrait briefs do not.
- Local typesetting with supplied fonts: Hiragino Sans GB rendered Chinese title/body into transparent type and measurable composite. Glyph coverage was checked and missing glyphs rejected, not dropped. The source heading mentioned system fonts historically; current workflow prohibits system fallback.
- Missing typography evidence caused check_poster to return needs verification rather than pass. Even 100 points could not override a typography hard failure.
- build_prompt was recorded as deterministic and language-independent: same brief produced identical output; English/Chinese twin difference was the language-appropriate typography description, with other keys including negative rules aligned. This historical claim still needs reproducible artifacts.
- Supplying style contact sheets/originals with composition images appeared to transfer palette/material/framing more effectively than adjectives alone; previews visibly followed the chosen style.

### Previously recorded problems

- Models combined separately requested A/B/C into triptychs/contact sheets. Existing remedies: explicit single-original-poster wording and one call per direction.
- Portrait habits leaked into landscape: empty side wings/letterboxing or vertical information stacks. Existing remedy: landscape block and explicit confirmed-ratio instructions.
- Integrated Chinese produced wrong/lookalike characters, extra punctuation, omissions despite exact prompt copy. Character-level final review and optional overlay/composite address this.
- Undeclared reference roles pulled composition away from approved layout. Existing remedies: explicit style/layout roles and supplied photography as subject base.
- Text-free backgrounds still generated decorative pseudo-letters. Explicit no text/pseudo-text/placeholders plus negative rules address this.
- Assuming local font availability failed on other machines. Bundled/installed-font approaches were abandoned; language-based pairing and supplied-font-only overlay now apply.
- Official designations were paraphrased/independently translated. The hard exact-string requirement with supplied language counterparts addresses this.

### Previously recorded untested items

- Real-poster composite production: unit tests reportedly covered neutral backgrounds, invalid-layer rejection, overwrite refusal, reproducible hashes, but no full delivery-resolution task was linked.
- Composite title ratio may exceed the 15% target after scaling into a region; measure final pixels, not layer dimensions.
- Vertical-title width measurement on landscape had unit coverage but no linked real layout validation.
- One to three references: stronger transfer versus composition drift was not measured.
- English integrated text appeared plausible but was not measured; Chinese lookalike errors do not establish English behavior.
