# Cultural event categories, layouts, and composition routing

Read at the start of step 4. First route an untidy brief to a visual relationship, then establish its binding category pool. Classify actual purpose/content, not generic cultural labels. Resolve paths from the skill root.

## 1. Authority and reading order

- Sections 2–5 route: what relationship carries this poster? They narrow candidates, not decide.
- Section 7 supplies binding category eligibility. Intersect routing suggestions with category pools and global exclusions; out-of-pool suggestions describe relationships only. This is a human-readable mirror; the machine authority is selection-guide R7, parsed by build_prompt.py.
- Selection-guide §§3–4 filter; actual style definitions and inspected references remain necessary. Information importance never waives typography caps.
- Read §2 first. New empirical evidence belongs in test-learnings, not this routing document.

## 2. Four questions

| # | Question | Branch |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | One thing, many parallel things, or relationships between things? | One: single/typographic/symbolic routes; many: parallel/festival routes; relationships: comparison/space/transformation/framing |
| Q2 | Static or moving? | Static: collection/structure/archive; moving: direction/gathering |
| Q3 | How much required copy? | Sparse: open-space routes; dense: columns/grouping/capacity checks |
| Q4 | Quiet collection-like reading or live/expressive activity? | Quiet: clusters 1–5; expressive may permit 6–7 |

Q1/Q4 are decisive. Q4 is permission, not preference. Energetic/experimental clusters require explicit preference or suitable music/live/experimental context. Exhibition/museum/heritage A/B/C must include one spacious restrained-color direction, following selection-guide's spectrum.

## 3. Content situation to composition

| Situation | Relationship | Starting layouts | Cluster |
|---|---|---|---|
| One work/artifact/object carries the focus | Isolated, complete subject | `centered-hero`, `off-center-hero` | 1, 5 |
| Making, hands, materials, process | Material layers and traces; people policy still applies | `layered-depth`, `frame-within-frame` | 3 |
| Parallel works/sessions/programs | Equal units with order | `modular-grid` | 2 |
| Performance/rhythm/program over time | Direction, flow, convergence | `diagonal-dynamic`, `radial-composition` | 6 |
| Curatorial text, talk, research | Continuous reading column | `editorial-column`, `split-vertical` | 2 |
| Two eras/viewpoints or scene/detail | Contrast and division | `split-horizontal`, `split-vertical` | 2 |
| Place/system/structure/route | Space and projection | `axonometric-spatial`, `large-geometry` | 4 |
| Folding/unfolding/transformation | Continuous changing plane | `folded-space`, `layered-depth` | 3, 7 |
| Immersive scene with text | Full image and quiet overlay | `full-bleed-image-overlay` with profile-compliant low-detail areas | 5 |
| Minimal assets, symbol, quiet idea | Space and symbolism | `minimal-white-space`, `large-geometry` | 1 |
| Deliberate nonlinear statement | Intentional disorder | `disorder-layout` | 7 |
| Festival/multiple programs/participation | Diversity and gathering | `modular-grid`, `radial-composition`, `diagonal-dynamic` | 6 |
| Window/case/lens/boundary | Framed viewing | `frame-within-frame` | 5, 1 |
| Letterforms are the concept | Type as image, not plain title | `large-geometry`, `off-center-hero` with typographic styles | 4, 6 |

Original/rendered lettering may be a subject, but event copy still follows the profile. big-type-only remains ineligible except the explicit free-design override defined in the main skill. Routing suggestions never restore excluded panels.

For exhibitions/performance/spatial work, favor eligible depth routes. A/B/C must differ in foreground/midground/background, occlusion/interweaving, light/medium, or material thickness, not merely palette.

## 5. Tie-breakers

Compare in order: required-copy fit above readable floor; actual inspected references; three spatially distinct title/information areas; fewer unsupported assumptions about facts/subject/channel. Do not default to centered-hero/editorial-column merely because a theme seems cultural, treat routing as a checklist, or override explicit layout choices.

## 6. Event categories

Use selection-guide R6: A static visual art; B performance/dynamic art; C heritage/traditional crafts; D knowledge/academic; E festivals/multicultural; F contemporary/experimental; G museums/collections; H spatial/architectural culture. The original work, movement, material, reading, participation, concept, artifact, or spatial structure respectively anchors the visual.

## 8. Usage rules

All routes inherit the active profile and global exclusions. Categories are starting candidates, not style or size prescriptions. D's information importance does not waive caps.

1. Explicit user instruction > category mapping > relationship refinement. Prefer main-category primaries, then specialists/secondaries. General preferences do not invalidate legitimate primaries, e.g. G permits centered artifacts.
2. A/B/C should use different eligible primaries and distinct compositions/text zones. With two primaries, normally use one each then a different specialist/secondary. Fixed user layouts prevail; vary other relationships independently.
3. Merge pools for mixed events and record main/secondary categories. A craft market may be C main + E secondary. Do not replace with a universal trio.
