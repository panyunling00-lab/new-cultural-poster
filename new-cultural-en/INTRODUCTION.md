# Cultural Event Poster Generator

**A reference-guided AI skill for designing culturally grounded event posters through a collaborative, review-driven workflow.**

`new-cultural-en` helps an AI assistant turn supplied event content and verified cultural context into posters for exhibitions, performances, heritage crafts, workshops, and festivals. It combines 38 visual styles, 17 layout definitions, visual reference libraries, and two typography profiles with a structured process: confirm the copy, choose a typography direction, compare three distinct composition drafts, and refine the selected design into a complete poster.

The skill emphasizes exact wording, cultural accuracy, faithful use of supplied assets, and inspection of the actual image. Supporting Python tools assemble prompts, validate briefs, track approval states, composite text and assets, and evaluate documented quality evidence. Image generation and cultural research are provided by the host assistant and its connected tools. Ordinary product advertising is outside its scope.

## Short description

Create cultural event posters from supplied content and verified context, with reference-guided art direction, three composition drafts, user-led selection, typography controls, and evidence-based quality review.
