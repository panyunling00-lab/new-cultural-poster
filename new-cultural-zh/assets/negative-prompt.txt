# Global negative prompt list — cultural event posters
#
# One rule per line, written as: <Chinese> | <English>
# `scripts/build_prompt.py` reads this file and appends one language's half to
# every image prompt (English by default; the Chinese half when the brief sets
# "language": "zh"). Keep both halves faithful to each other — they are the
# same rule, not a longer and a shorter version. Lines starting with # are
# comments and are skipped. No "|" may appear inside a rule.
#
# Typography profiles: every rule here is profile-neutral and carries no
# profile-specific number. The two profiles are design-free-v1
# (assets/typography-design-free.json, the default) and small-type-refined-v1
# (assets/typography-small-type.json); the brief selects one with
# typography_profile. The profile-violation rule below applies to whichever
# profile is active. The multi-panel rule is unconditional. The people rule is
# conditional: it holds except for performing-arts (B), festival/carnival (E)
# or market themes, or when the user's reference material contains only people;
# scripts/build_prompt.py resolves this as people_policy and also injects the
# matching positive directive into every image prompt.
#
# This file is byte-identical in the English and the Chinese skill packages: it
# is one shared asset, not a translation pair.
#
# 每行一条规则，格式为：<中文> | <英文>。
# `scripts/build_prompt.py` 读取本文件，并把其中一种语言的一半追加到每个生图提示词
# （默认英文；当设计简报设置 "language": "zh" 时用中文）。两侧必须互相忠实——它们
# 是同一条规则，而不是一长一短两个版本。以 # 开头的行为注释，会被跳过；规则内部
# 不得出现 "|"。
#
# 排版档位：本清单全部档位中立，不含任何档位专属数字。两个档位为 design-free-v1
# （assets/typography-design-free.json，默认档）与 small-type-refined-v1
# （assets/typography-small-type.json）；设计简报通过 typography_profile 选择。
# 下文的「违反当前排版档位」一条按当前档位生效。分格拼版一条为无条件全局规则；
# 人物一条为条件规则——除演出（B）、节庆/嘉年华（E）、集市类主题，或用户参考素材
# 仅含人物外成立；scripts/build_prompt.py 将其解析为 people_policy，并把对应的
# 正向人物指令一并注入每个生图提示词。
#
# 本文件在英文包与中文包中逐字节相同：它是同一份共享资产，不是翻译对。
编造未提供或未核实的活动名称、日期、地点、费用、嘉宾、参与方式、场次、票价、优惠、背书，或编造文化归属、历史来源、宗教含义、遗产身份、奖项与机构背书 | Invented or unverified event names, dates, venues, fees, guests, participation details, sessions, prices, discounts, endorsements, or invented cultural attribution, historical origins, religious meanings, heritage status, awards or institutional endorsements
擅自改写必须原样呈现的正式名称与指定文案 | Unauthorized changes to mandatory official names or exact supplied copy
未经提供或核实的电影节桂冠、奖项徽章、认证、“official selection”或“入围”字样 | Unsupplied or unverified film-festival laurels, award badges, certifications, "official selection" or "selected" wording
真实品牌、真实赞助商或真实机构标识（任务提供并要求原样合成的除外） | Real brand marks, real sponsors or real institution logos (except assets the task supplies for unchanged compositing)

把风格参考或概念性场景当作真实展品、活动现场、历史复原或主办方资产 | Style references or conceptual scenes presented as actual exhibits, event scenes, historical reconstructions or organizer assets

不可读的主标题，或变形到无法辨认、笔画粘连的字形 | Unreadable titles, or glyphs distorted beyond recognition with fused strokes
违反当前排版档位的字号、字重、间距、边界或遮挡，含放大到主导整个画面的超大标题 | Type sizes, weights, spacing, bounds or occlusion violating the active typography profile, including oversized titles that dominate the whole image

以删必需信息或不可读微字解决版面容量问题 | Solving layout capacity by dropping required information or using unreadable microtype
重绘、改风格或重新生成用户提供的 logo、二维码与实拍照片，改变需保真作品、器物或照片的关键识别特征，或以生成近似物替代必需原始资产、遮挡或拉伸变形二维码 | Redrawing, restyling or regenerating user-supplied logos, QR codes or photographs, altering identifying features of fidelity-sensitive works, objects or photographs, or substituting generated approximations for required original assets and obscuring or stretching QR codes out of shape
堆砌俗套国潮纹样、模板化“中国风”符号，或无关文化符号、错误时代地域混搭与无依据的纹样堆砌 | Piled-on clichéd guochao motifs, template "Chinese-style" symbols, unrelated cultural symbols, incorrect period or regional mixtures or unsupported motif piles

文字上的浮雕、描边或阴影，导致可读性下降 | Emboss, stroke or shadow on text that reduces readability
多种高饱和色等面积冲撞，或把糖果色、荧光色用在传统文化主题上 | Equal-area clashes of saturated colors, or candy and fluorescent colors on traditional cultural themes
超出“背景＋主色＋点缀”三个主要色彩角色之外的高饱和色 | Saturated colors beyond the three major roles of background, primary and optional accent
水印、署名条、编造的制作名单，画框、桌面、手持等展示样机，或未要求的多海报拼图 | Watermarks, credit strips, invented production credits, presentation mockups such as frames, desks or hands, or unrequested multi-poster montages
把竖版构图逻辑塞进横向画布：黑边、空白侧翼、堆叠的竖版栏 | Portrait composition logic forced into a landscape canvas: letterbox bars, empty side wings, stacked portrait columns
