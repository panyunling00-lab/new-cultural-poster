# Centered Hero

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Use Cases

Use for cultural events centered on a single artifact, object, person, or artwork. Suitable for exhibitions with low to moderate information density and a strong visual subject.

## Selection Signals

Choose when one clearly identifiable subject carries the theme, high-quality hero imagery is available, and viewers should recognize the subject immediately.

## Composition Structure

- Place the dominant subject at or near the optical center.
- Position the headline above, below, or in a close relationship with the subject.
- Use stable, symmetrical or nearly symmetrical balance.
- Group dates, venue, and organizer information in a footer or corner.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. The hero subject provides the first read; a separate small title identifies the event.
2. A subtitle or theme statement supports understanding.
3. Date, venue, and organizational details form the third level.

## Text Placement

- Use a small refined headline with complete supplied copy.
- Keep type away from essential features of the subject.
- Group supporting information and maintain a clear relationship between text and image.

## Image Treatment

Use a recognizable subject with a clear silhouette. Simplify or soften the background to strengthen separation. Keep one visual focal point.

## Spacing Rules

Leave generous space around the hero. Use negative space to emphasize its importance; do not fill every corner with decoration.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Strong in `2:3`, `3:4`, `4:5`, and `9:16`. In landscape layouts, enlarge or extend the hero and move text into side areas.

## Avoid

- Multiple competing subjects.
- Overly complex backgrounds or dense information.
- Mechanical center symmetry without visual variation.
