# Radial Composition

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Use Cases

Use for traditional culture, festivals, celebrations, and exhibitions with a central theme or ceremonial character. Suitable for gathering, cultural dissemination, energy expansion, cycles, and traditional patterns.

## Selection Signals

Choose when there is a clear central core and elements naturally converge toward it or spread outward. Appropriate for themes of inheritance, convergence, cosmology, or cyclical development, and for a center-to-periphery reading sequence.

## Composition Structure

- Place the core visual at the center.
- Arrange supporting elements in rings, rays, or concentric layers.
- Vary size, distance, and direction to express expansion or convergence.
- Keep the center clearly dominant over the periphery.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. Central subject establishes the first focal point.
2. Surrounding elements reinforce meaning and visual layers.
3. Peripheral text and event details form the supporting level.

## Text Placement

- Place the title at the center, around it, or on a circular path.
- Keep supporting information toward the outer areas.
- Maintain coherent text orientation and protect the central focus.

## Image Treatment

Use circular patterns, architectural structures, grouped figures, radiating light, or natural forms with a central organization. Repeat, rotate, or grade elements while retaining the greatest visual weight at the center.

## Spacing Rules

Keep the center focused and leave space between peripheral elements. Avoid compressing the entire radial structure against the canvas edges.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Works in `1:1`, `2:3`, `3:4`, and `4:5`. Square formats best express radial organization; portrait formats can extend layers upward and downward.

## Avoid

- An unclear center.
- Too many radiating elements.
- Circular decoration without thematic meaning.
- Equal visual weight at the center and periphery.
