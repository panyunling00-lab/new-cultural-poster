# Editorial Column

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Use Cases

Use for museum, art, design, and cultural research events with multiple layers of information. Suitable for moderate content density and a reading experience inspired by magazines, exhibition guides, or curatorial texts.

## Selection Signals

Choose when title, introduction, artwork descriptions, date, and venue require distinct but connected treatment. Appropriate when explanation matters alongside imagery and content needs a continuous reading path.

## Composition Structure

- Organize images, headlines, and copy into vertical columns.
- Define primary visual, title, and supporting information areas.
- Use a grid, column widths, alignment, and negative space to establish order.
- Connect columns into a continuous reading sequence, like an exhibition catalog.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. The graphic/illustration establishes the entry point; the title identifies the event.
2. Introduction, subtitle, or theme text explains the content.
3. Artwork information, dates, venue, and organizer details form supporting modules.

## Text Placement

- Use defined column widths and consistent alignment.
- Distinguish title, body, and metadata through size, weight, and spacing.
- Preserve all body copy as readable typesetting; avoid fragmentation and re-layout when needed.

## Image Treatment

Use photography, artwork crops, artifact details, or abstract graphics. Images may occupy independent columns or combine with copy; they should communicate content rather than serve as background decoration.

## Spacing Rules

Use generous space to separate modules and create a refined editorial feel. Avoid accumulating information without clear intervals.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Works in `2:3`, `3:4`, and `4:5`. In landscape, expand into a wider multi-column structure.

## Avoid

- A generic promotional flyer with no editorial hierarchy.
- Too many columns or unclear information levels.
- Long body copy that removes the visual focus.
