# Glitch Art

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Create an experimental digital poster that transforms image corruption, signal interference, and technological malfunction into an expressive visual language. Explore pixel displacement, RGB channel separation, fragmented imagery, digital compression artifacts, and disrupted interface structures. Treat glitches as intentional compositional elements that reveal the instability of digital media. Balance recognizable visual content with controlled technological disruption, creating tension between digital precision and system failure without relying on arbitrary visual noise.

## Typography

- Use bold grotesk sans-serif, monospaced lettering, bitmap fonts, pixel-inspired typography, or clean digital-era display type.
- Experiment with horizontal text displacement, duplicated letterforms, chromatic ghosting, fragmented words, and interrupted baselines.
- Allow selected characters or text layers to split, repeat, shift, or partially dissolve into digital artifacts.
- Explore typography affected by scanline interference, pixel fragmentation, data-like repetition, and localized signal distortion.
- Contrast corrupted experimental headlines with stable, precisely aligned supporting typography.
- Preserve accurate spelling and readable essential information, keeping dates, locations, and key messages outside heavily distorted regions.

## Palette

Favor digital signal-inspired palettes: black, white, cobalt blue, electric cyan, magenta, ultraviolet, acid green, and signal red. Explore RGB channel displacement, chromatic aberration, iridescent interference, and pale spectral gradients. Use dark digital backgrounds for intense electronic disruption or light neutral backgrounds for refined experimental compositions. Let color distortion emerge from simulated signal errors rather than arbitrary decorative gradients.

## Imagery

Use digitally fragmented portraits, distorted human figures, corrupted photographs, displaced image strips, pixelated silhouettes, broken screen captures, fragmented interface windows, abstract data patterns, and signal interference fields. Explore horizontal image tearing, pixel sorting, compression block displacement, duplicated visual fragments, and chromatic channel separation. Allow recognizable imagery to transition into abstract digital artifacts while retaining a meaningful relationship to the original subject.

## Texture / Material

Digital scanlines, pixel noise, JPEG compression blocks, chromatic aberration, RGB ghosting, datamoshing-like smearing, raster displacement, screen interference, corrupted image fragments, and low-resolution display textures. Combine sharp digital edges with selectively broken or displaced image regions. Use electronic noise and compression artifacts to reinforce the malfunction concept without overwhelming the entire composition.

## Composition Rules

- Begin with one recognizable digital image, typographic structure, or interface-based visual system as the foundation.
- Select one dominant glitch mechanism, such as horizontal tearing, pixel displacement, signal interference, color-channel separation, or data fragmentation.
- Apply technological disruption selectively, creating contrast between intact visual regions and corrupted image areas.
- Use displaced image strips, repeated fragments, broken grids, and interrupted visual continuity to establish rhythm and tension.
- Explore transitions between recognizable photographic subjects and abstract digital distortion.
- Combine experimental glitch imagery with stable typography, intentional negative space, and clearly organized information zones.
- Allow visual errors to interrupt image boundaries, overlap typographic elements, or fragment spatial relationships while preserving overall compositional coherence.
- Make each glitch effect consistent with a recognizable digital malfunction or signal-processing logic rather than random decorative distortion.

## Avoid

Random visual noise, excessive RGB splitting, generic cyberpunk effects, decorative static overlays, meaningless pixel fragmentation, uniformly corrupted imagery, excessive neon gradients, unreadable primary messages, arbitrary interface clutter, unrelated retro computer icons, glossy science-fiction rendering, print-like distress without digital logic, and compositions in which technological errors lack a clear visual or conceptual purpose.
