# Swiss Editorial

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Create a refined, graphic-led poster with small refined typography with strict grid discipline, asymmetry, clarity, and modern editorial restraint.

## Typography

- Use clean neo-grotesk or grotesk sans-serif direction.
- Favor strong scale contrast: small refined headline, compact metadata.
- Use left alignment, asymmetric placement, and disciplined baseline rhythm.
- Avoid decorative fonts and excessive type effects.

## Palette

Neutral base: white, warm white, black, charcoal, gray. Add one precise accent such as red, cobalt, acid green, or orange.

## Imagery

Use one restrained image, abstract crop, simple object, or no image. Imagery carries the visual hierarchy; the small text follows the grid in quiet safe areas.

## Texture / Material

Mostly clean matte paper or subtle print grain. Keep surface treatment minimal.

## Composition Rules

- Use visible or implied grid columns.
- Keep text blocks aligned and intentional.
- Use rules, numbers, or small labels sparingly.

## Avoid

Centered generic flyer layouts, random decoration, too many colors, fake Swiss clichés, illegible tiny type, and cluttered backgrounds.
