# Kinetic Typography

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Make lettering a designed moving form whose path, scale and rhythm express the theme.

## Typography

Use letter-by-letter shifts, curved paths, controlled stretching and variable rhythm; retain every required character and correct reading order.

## Palette

Favor bold, high-contrast color combinations that reinforce motion and visual energy: black, white, off-white, acid yellow, electric orange, vivid red, cobalt blue, and bright lime. Use monochromatic schemes for refined spatial movement or saturated accent colors for energetic compositions. Keep the palette controlled so that typography remains the primary visual focus.

## Imagery

Use a thematic moving subject, curved paths, fine motion trajectories or directional geometry as the main visual. Do not repeat event phrases into large lettering tunnels or ribbons.

## Texture / Material

Crisp vector typography, clean flat ink, subtle paper grain, fine linear patterns, controlled motion blur, optical line density, and restrained print textures. Favor sharp, well-defined letterforms combined with precise linear transitions. Use blur, grain, or visual trails selectively to emphasize directional movement rather than obscure the typographic structure.

## Composition Rules

- Establish theme-related motion through designed lettering and graphic trajectories.
- Allow large type, interleaving and partial occlusion when readable.
- Keep functional copy accessible; no mandatory grid, margins or type-size ratios.

## Avoid

Randomly rotated text, meaningless perspective distortion, chaotic letter repetition, excessive motion blur, unreadable primary messages, inconsistent movement directions, decorative speed lines without purpose, unnecessary 3D extrusion, excessive visual effects, cluttered backgrounds, weak motion hierarchy, and typography that appears distorted without communicating a clear sense of movement.
