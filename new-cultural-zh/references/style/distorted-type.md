# Distorted Type

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Use a theme-related distorted graphic form to express movement or instability; event copy stays small and readable in safe regions.

## Typography

Design letterforms with theme-led scale, distortion, layering and unequal rhythm. Use typography-moves.md as optional inspiration. Preserve all required characters and normal-size readability; large or interleaved text is allowed.

## Palette

Favor high-contrast palettes built around black, white, and off-white, with expressive accents such as hot pink, electric blue, vivid red, ultraviolet, or spectral gradients. Use monochromatic schemes for structural distortion, or controlled multicolor effects for optical and chromatic deformation. Let color reinforce the transformation of typography rather than compete with its shape.

## Imagery

Apply controlled stretching, fragmentation or optical deformation to a thematic graphic/illustration; protect event text from distortion, blur and occlusion.

## Texture / Material

Digital blur, chromatic aberration, ink diffusion, spray-like grain, halftone noise, photocopy degradation, pixel dispersion, rough letter edges, and subtle analog print texture. Combine crisp vector letterforms with softened, eroded, or fragmented surfaces. Use texture selectively to communicate the physical or perceptual transformation of the type.

## Composition Rules

- Lettering can be the principal visual object.
- Use the selected deformation/material mechanism on letterforms, not just background decoration.
- Allow full-bleed overlay, uneven placement and legible occlusion.
- Functional copy remains accessible; no size, area or negative-space caps.

## Avoid

Arbitrary font warping, meaningless character deformation, unreadable primary messages, accidental misspellings, random typographic fragments, excessive glitch effects, uncontrolled blur, decorative distortion without conceptual purpose, unnecessary 3D extrusion, generic psychedelic lettering, overly complicated backgrounds, and visual effects that obscure the essential identity of the type.
