# Retro Futurism

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Create a nostalgic-yet-modern poster blending vintage space-age optimism, analog technology, warm gradients, and contemporary polish.

## Typography

- Use rounded geometric type, vintage sci-fi display, or clean sans-serif with retro spacing.
- Keep typography bold and readable.
- Use small metadata like a vintage label or mission tag.

## Palette

Burnt orange, cream, teal, midnight blue, mustard, dusty pink, chrome, warm sunset gradients.

## Imagery

Planets, orbits, grids, retro screens, chrome devices, star fields, sunrise horizons, analog machinery.

## Texture / Material

Film grain, airbrush gradients, screenprint dots, old paper, chrome, CRT glow.

## Composition Rules

- Combine vintage motifs with modern spacing.
- Use one strong retro-future symbol.
- Keep the poster optimistic, cinematic, and clean.
- Avoid pure nostalgia by preserving contemporary hierarchy.

## Avoid

Overcrowded space clichés, unreadable novelty fonts, excessive stars, cheap synthwave tropes, and muddy retro colors.
