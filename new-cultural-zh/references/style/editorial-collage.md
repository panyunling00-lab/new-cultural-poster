# Editorial Collage

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Create a contemporary magazine-style poster with layered photography, cutouts, typography fragments, and curated editorial tension.

## Typography

- Use bold editorial serif/sans contrast or confident magazine display type.
- Combine large headline with smaller caption-like labels.
- Allow controlled overlap between type and image fragments.
- Keep required details in a clean label or footer.

## Palette

Neutral editorial base with one or two accents: black, cream, off-white, red, cobalt, olive, metallic gray.

## Imagery

Layer cutout photography, torn paper, art scans, object fragments, annotations, and cropped portraits.

## Texture / Material

Paper tear, tape, scan grain, photocopy, subtle shadow, matte editorial paper.

## Composition Rules

- Use one dominant cutout or headline as anchor.
- Let layers create depth while preserving reading zones.
- Balance dense collage with open negative space.
- Make every fragment feel selected, not random.

## Avoid

Random scrapbook clutter, too many unrelated fragments, messy shadows, illegible details, and fake magazine mastheads unless requested.
