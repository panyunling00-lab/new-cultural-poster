# Xerox Punk

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Create a raw, rebellious, DIY-inspired poster rooted in underground punk zines, photocopied flyers, and independent print culture. Combine high-contrast imagery, rough cut-and-paste typography, degraded reproduction, and spontaneous graphic interventions to establish an urgent, confrontational visual identity. Embrace the imperfections of low-cost analog reproduction, allowing photocopy artifacts, uneven layering, and handmade construction to become essential expressive elements rather than decorative effects.

## Typography

- Use heavy condensed sans-serif, distressed display type, stencil lettering, typewriter fonts, or irregular cutout typography.
- Combine contrasting letter sizes, weights, orientations, and type styles to create an improvised paste-up appearance.
- Experiment with designed headlines, ransom-note-style lettering, uneven baselines, tilted text strips, and roughly cropped characters.
- Allow letters to overlap, interrupt, or partially obscure photographic elements while preserving the main message.
- Use photocopied labels, stamped text, handwritten annotations, and compact typewritten information blocks as supporting typography.
- Maintain recognizable headlines and legible essential information despite the intentionally chaotic arrangement.

## Palette

Favor stark photocopy-inspired combinations of toner black, paper white, dirty gray, and aged off-white. Introduce one or two aggressive accent colors when appropriate: fluorescent pink, acid yellow, vivid red, or electric green. Use saturated colors as flat overlays, printed interventions, or highlighted text strips. Preserve the dominance of harsh black-and-white contrast rather than relying on polished multicolor palettes.

## Imagery

Use high-contrast photocopied portraits, underground music photography, punk fashion imagery, newspaper fragments, magazine cutouts, silhouettes, xerox-degraded illustrations, hand-drawn marks, torn photographs, and rough graphic symbols. Explore fragmented faces, repeated image fragments, irregular black shapes, expressive ink splatters, and layered found imagery. Favor direct, imperfect image treatments that evoke independently assembled zines and street-distributed flyers.

## Texture / Material

Photocopier toner grain, harsh threshold contrast, crushed blacks, blown-out highlights, halftone degradation, ink bleeding, paper tears, rough cut edges, tape marks, scratches, scan noise, toner streaks, and repeated-copy artifacts. Use uneven print density, misaligned layers, and visible paper textures to reinforce the physical reproduction process. Keep textures gritty, tactile, and imperfect rather than digitally polished.

## Composition Rules

- Begin with one confrontational headline, expressive portrait, or dominant graphic fragment as the visual anchor.
- Build the composition through overlapping photocopied images, irregular paper cutouts, and pasted typographic fragments.
- Use strong black-and-white contrast, aggressive scale shifts, and uneven alignment to create visual tension.
- Combine dense collage regions with strategically preserved reading zones for essential event information.
- Introduce handwritten marks, rough strokes, torn edges, or graphic interruptions to emphasize the DIY production process.
- Allow spontaneous-looking asymmetry, cropped imagery, and imperfect registration while retaining a deliberate overall hierarchy.
- Use bright accent colors selectively to amplify focal points or create an additional printed layer.
- Make the poster feel physically assembled, repeatedly copied, and distributed through an independent underground culture.

## Avoid

Clean corporate layouts, polished commercial photography, smooth gradients, glossy 3D effects, overly refined typography, sterile vector perfection, decorative grunge filters without material logic, excessive unrelated collage fragments, artificial distress applied uniformly, generic punk stereotypes, random aggressive slogans, illegible essential information, and compositions that imitate photocopy aesthetics without conveying the rough physicality of DIY print production.
