# Dreamcore–Softcore

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Create an atmospheric, emotionally evocative poster inspired by dreams, fragmented memories, nostalgic imagery, and subtly surreal environments. Transform familiar landscapes, everyday objects, and ordinary spaces into quiet, uncanny, or gently fantastical visual experiences. Explore the boundary between reality and imagination through impossible spatial relationships, softened light, exaggerated natural scenery, and dreamlike photographic treatments. Balance comforting familiarity with subtle strangeness, allowing the composition to evoke a sense of wonder, longing, solitude, or suspended time without relying on conventional fantasy illustration.

## Typography

- Use clean contemporary sans-serif, soft geometric typography, delicate serif lettering, or expressive calligraphic display type according to the emotional tone.
- Favor understated typographic hierarchy, generous letter spacing, softly integrated headlines, and small editorial captions.
- Experiment with small translucent lettering, repeated poetic phrases, elegant script overlays, and text positioned within open atmospheric spaces.
- Allow typography to interact with skies, clouds, landscapes, and isolated photographic subjects without dominating the dreamlike imagery.
- Use contrasting type styles selectively to evoke nostalgia, intimacy, or the fragmented quality of memory.
- Keep essential information readable while allowing secondary text to feel quiet, distant, or integrated into the visual atmosphere.

## Palette

Favor emotionally expressive palettes: powder blue, deep cobalt, twilight navy, cloud white, pastel pink, lavender, soft cyan, mint green, lush grass green, and muted cream. Explore atmospheric gradients, gentle color transitions, luminous highlights, and slightly unnatural landscape saturation. Use warm pink clouds against cool blue skies or vibrant green scenery against soft cyan backgrounds. For nocturnal interpretations, favor deep black, faded gray, and isolated overexposed white light. Allow color to establish the emotional temperature of the dream rather than following a fixed pastel aesthetic.

## Imagery

Use floating beds, isolated houses, suburban streets, endless skies, oversized clouds, suspended objects, grassy hills, quiet bodies of water, nostalgic domestic environments, glowing animals, and solitary human figures. Explore familiar environments transformed by impossible geography, weightlessness, altered scale, unexpected isolation, or atmospheric displacement. Combine photographic realism with subtle surreal interventions, allowing everyday objects and landscapes to appear strangely peaceful, emotionally charged, or detached from ordinary reality.

## Texture / Material

Soft photographic grain, analog film noise, slight blur, atmospheric haze, overexposed highlights, faded print textures, gentle bloom, subtle chromatic softness, and dreamlike light diffusion. Combine recognizable photographic detail with selective low-fidelity reproduction or smooth digitally constructed scenery. Allow slightly imperfect image surfaces to reinforce nostalgia and memory. Preserve atmospheric depth and material believability while avoiding excessively sharp, sterile, or glossy rendering.

## Composition Rules

- Begin with one recognizable environment, everyday object, or emotionally resonant image as the visual foundation.
- Introduce a subtle but meaningful surreal transformation, such as floating, impossible elevation, unusual scale, spatial isolation, or altered environmental relationships.
- Use expansive skies, open landscapes, deep shadows, or atmospheric negative space to create stillness and emotional distance.
- Establish a clear focal point through isolated objects, luminous figures, suspended elements, or unexpected spatial contrasts.
- Balance realistic photographic cues with impossible spatial conditions so that the scene feels familiar yet distinctly dreamlike.
- Use soft transitions, gentle light diffusion, exaggerated environmental color, and atmospheric depth to establish a coherent emotional tone.
- Integrate typography as a secondary narrative layer, preserving the visual dominance of the dreamscape or surreal image.
- Allow both luminous, comforting dream environments and darker, melancholic interpretations while maintaining a restrained and evocative visual language.
- Ensure that surreal elements reinforce an emotional or conceptual idea rather than functioning as random fantasy decoration.

## Avoid

Generic fantasy landscapes, excessive magical effects, decorative pastel gradients without atmosphere, random floating objects, overly cute cartoon imagery, stereotypical fairy-tale motifs, heavy horror aesthetics, aggressive visual clutter, excessive neon lighting, glossy science-fiction rendering, hyper-detailed fantasy architecture, incoherent surreal object combinations, overly polished stock photography, and compositions that lack a recognizable emotional or spatial concept.
