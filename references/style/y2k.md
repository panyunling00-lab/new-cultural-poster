# Y2K

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Create a nostalgic yet futuristic poster inspired by late-1990s and early-2000s digital culture, technological optimism, and experimental graphic design. Combine retro-digital typography, glossy or translucent materials, early computer graphics, and expressive pop-cultural imagery to evoke the visual imagination of the millennium era. Explore the relationship between emerging technology, consumer electronics, fashion, and playful digital expression. Balance futuristic experimentation with nostalgic analog-digital imperfections, creating a distinctive visual identity without relying on generic cyberpunk aesthetics.

## Typography

- Use rounded techno sans-serif, futuristic geometric type, extended display lettering, pixel-inspired accents, or experimental digital-era typography.
- Explore wide letter spacing, modular glyph construction, unconventional character proportions, outlined lettering, and compact technical labels.
- Combine small futuristic headlines with small editorial captions, numerical codes, and interface-inspired metadata.
- Allow typography to interact with photography, translucent surfaces, digital grids, and abstract technological forms.
- Experiment with horizontal and vertical arrangements, layered outlines, and selective typographic repetition.
- Preserve recognizable characters and readable information despite the experimental digital appearance.

## Palette

Favor millennium-inspired digital palettes: metallic silver, icy blue, aqua cyan, cobalt blue, electric purple, soft lavender, pearlescent white, and cool gray. Introduce saturated accents such as neon pink, lime green, bright orange, or vivid yellow when appropriate. Explore cool-toned monochromatic combinations, iridescent reflections, and controlled chromatic gradients. Allow brighter playful palettes for pop-cultural interpretations while maintaining a coherent retro-digital atmosphere.

## Imagery

Use early digital photography, futuristic fashion portraits, consumer electronics, game controllers, translucent plastic devices, chrome objects, water droplets, reflective surfaces, optical discs, abstract digital landscapes, interface-inspired symbols, and simplified pop illustrations. Explore image masking, technological iconography, fragmented digital graphics, atmospheric photography, geometric dot matrices, and layered photographic compositions. Favor distinctive millennium-era technological imagery over generic contemporary sci-fi visuals.

## Texture / Material

Chrome, brushed metal, translucent plastic, glossy resin, frosted glass, liquid reflections, pearlescent surfaces, low-resolution digital textures, pixel artifacts, subtle scanlines, analog photographic grain, and compressed image characteristics. Combine polished synthetic surfaces with restrained early-digital imperfections. Use reflections, transparency, and luminous material effects selectively to create tactile technological nostalgia.

## Composition Rules

- Establish a recognizable millennium-era visual concept through typography, technological imagery, or a distinctive material treatment.
- Combine futuristic letterforms with photographic elements, digital symbols, or synthetic surfaces as the main visual anchor.
- Explore asymmetric editorial layouts, modular information blocks, geometric grids, and layered image arrangements.
- Use transparency, reflective highlights, chrome accents, and cool-toned gradients to create an early-digital sense of depth.
- Introduce rounded rectangles, circles, dot matrices, fine rules, and interface-inspired graphic details where they support the concept.
- Balance visually rich technological imagery with clean negative space and clearly structured typographic information.
- Allow expressive pop-cultural graphics or nostalgic digital artifacts while maintaining a consistent visual direction.
- Select one dominant Y2K visual language—techno-fashion, liquid-digital, digital editorial, or playful pop—rather than combining every millennium-era motif.

## Avoid

Generic cyberpunk cityscapes, excessive neon lighting, random futuristic interfaces, fake interactive buttons, modern corporate technology templates, overcomplicated holographic effects, excessive chrome objects, unrelated nostalgic stickers, uncontrolled glitch noise, unreadable novelty fonts, overly polished science-fiction rendering, and stereotypical Y2K decorations without a coherent early-digital visual concept.
