# Webcore

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Intent

Create an expressive internet-inspired poster drawing from early personal websites, desktop operating systems, browser interfaces, and online subcultures of the late 1990s and early 2000s. Combine nostalgic digital imagery, layered interface fragments, pixel graphics, unconventional typography, and user-generated visual elements to establish a playful, fragmented, and distinctly online aesthetic. Explore the tension between functional computer interfaces and chaotic personal expression. Transform recognizable internet conventions into a visually compelling graphic composition rather than reproducing a literal or functional website.

## Typography

- Use web-safe sans-serif, pixel fonts, monospaced lettering, bitmap typography, retro-system fonts, or experimental techno display type.
- Combine designed headlines with small interface-style labels, underlined hyperlink-inspired text, system messages, and compact metadata.
- Experiment with irregular text placement, repeated phrases, mixed font weights, outlined lettering, and unconventional scale relationships.
- Allow typography to appear inside simulated browser windows, dialog boxes, navigation bars, digital labels, or overlapping interface fragments.
- Introduce decorative cyber lettering, chrome-like outlines, or compressed bitmap text when appropriate to the visual direction.
- Preserve clear information hierarchy and readable essential content despite the fragmented internet-inspired arrangement.

## Palette

Favor early-web and desktop-inspired color combinations: electric blue, cobalt blue, cyan, bright white, silver gray, classic interface gray, black, and deep navy. Introduce vivid accent colors such as neon green, magenta, bright yellow, red, or aqua. Explore both clean white-background digital collage and dense metallic-blue cyber-web palettes. Use flat interface colors, sharp contrast, and occasional chrome or pixelated gradient effects while maintaining visual coherence.

## Imagery

Use vintage desktop icons, browser windows, dialog boxes, cursor symbols, pixel illustrations, old mobile phones, portable music players, computer hardware, optical discs, web badges, digital stickers, low-resolution photographs, and early internet graphics. Explore layered interface screenshots, retro software elements, wireframe illustrations, techno ornaments, repeated digital symbols, and fragmented online imagery. Treat digital objects as graphic artifacts rather than functioning interface controls.

## Texture / Material

Pixelated edges, bitmap graphics, low-resolution raster textures, JPEG compression artifacts, subtle scanlines, early digital gradients, metallic interface borders, beveled buttons, translucent overlays, and screen-like image noise. Combine crisp vector typography with imperfect raster imagery and nostalgic software-rendered surfaces. Allow variations in image resolution and digital texture to suggest materials collected from different generations of online media.

## Composition Rules

- Begin with a recognizable internet-era visual concept, such as a desktop environment, personal homepage, digital scrapbook, or cyber-web graphic system.
- Build the composition from layered digital images, interface fragments, typographic modules, and nostalgic technological objects.
- Experiment with window-like framing, overlapping panels, irregular grids, floating icons, and fragmented information structures.
- Create contrast between small typographic accents and small interface-inspired details, labels, or decorative graphics.
- Allow visually dense digital clusters while maintaining clear focal points and readable information zones.
- Use pixel motifs, cursor graphics, system borders, and hyperlink-inspired elements as part of a coherent visual language.
- Explore both restrained desktop collage and highly ornamented internet-maximalist layouts according to the desired mood.
- Make the composition feel assembled from recognizable digital-cultural artifacts while preserving the character of a designed static poster.

## Avoid

Generic futuristic technology posters, excessive cyberpunk neon, modern corporate UI templates, unrelated sticker clutter, arbitrary browser windows, fake functional controls, misleading interactive elements, random pixel decoration, uncontrolled glitch noise, excessive chrome effects, illegible interface text, meaningless technical data, inconsistent digital-era references, and compositions that imitate a screenshot without a clear graphic concept.
