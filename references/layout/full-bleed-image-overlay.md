# Full Bleed Image Overlay

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Use Cases

Use for art exhibitions, cultural events, brand launches, and thematic campaigns with strong photography, illustration, or artwork. Suitable for subjects, scenes, cultural symbols, and emotional themes requiring immediate impact.

## Selection Signals

Choose when a high-quality, complete image can communicate the theme across the entire canvas. Appropriate when text should integrate with imagery and the image can carry most of the meaning or mood.

## Composition Structure

- Extend the image across the entire canvas.
- Use it as the primary visual foundation.
- Overlay title and supporting copy directly on the image.
- Coordinate text position, scale, and opacity so image and typography form one composition.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. Main image attracts attention first.
2. Title combines with the image to identify the theme.
3. Date, venue, and event details form the supporting level.

## Text Placement

- Place the title in naturally open or low-detail image areas.
- Respond to image brightness when positioning and coloring text.
- Use contrast, shadows, outlines, or controlled opacity where needed for readability.
- Allow intentional subject-type overlap while preserving essential identity and readable copy.

## Image Treatment

Use a high-quality image suitable for large-area coverage. Crop, enlarge, adjust tone, or selectively soften it while keeping the image the primary visual source.

## Spacing Rules

Use natural negative space within the image for text. Text may overlay detailed imagery using designed contrast and legible interleaving; no fixed negative-space quota.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Works in `2:3`, `3:4`, `4:5`, `9:16`, and `16:9`. Adapt the crop and text zones to each format.

## Avoid

- Low-quality background images.
- Text and imagery competing for attention.
- Excessive information that damages image integrity.
- Text placed on top without a deliberate relationship to the image.
