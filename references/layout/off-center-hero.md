# Off-center Hero

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Use Cases

Use for art exhibitions, cultural exhibitions, and brand cultural events that need a contemporary or curatorial feel. Suitable when the subject and surrounding space should create visual tension.

## Selection Signals

Choose when the core subject is clear but a centered treatment feels too static, text needs its own spatial relationship with the image, and negative space should establish rhythm.

## Composition Structure

- Place the hero distinctly toward the left, right, or upper area.
- Use the opposite area for text.
- Balance the composition asymmetrically.
- Treat the subject and negative space as equally intentional parts of the layout.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. Off-center hero establishes the first focal point.
2. Headline creates the second relationship with the hero.
3. Supporting details occupy peripheral areas.

## Text Placement

- Position the headline in response to the subject.
- Use negative space as a coherent text zone.
- Keep text blocks intact rather than scattering individual details.

## Image Treatment

Crop or enlarge the subject where useful. Emphasize its silhouette and form against a simple background.

## Spacing Rules

Use negative space as a primary design element. Leave empty areas unfilled when they strengthen the subject and visual rhythm.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Especially effective in portrait `2:3`, `3:4`, and `4:5`. In landscape, enlarge the hero and establish a clear left–right relationship with text.

## Avoid

- An offset too slight to create a meaningful relationship.
- Empty space without a compositional purpose.
- Text and subject competing for attention.
