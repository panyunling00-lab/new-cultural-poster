# Axonometric Spatial

> **Typography profile:** read [typography-constraints.md](../typography-constraints.md) first. Type scale, density, margins, negative space and hierarchy come only from the active profile: `assets/typography-defaults.json` names the default profile and links its file, and the numbers live in that profile's own file under `assets/`. Under `small-type-refined-v1` the profile's caps override any scale, density, type-dominance, cropping or edge-placement wording below; under `design-free-v1` this definition applies as written.

## Use Cases

Use for architecture, cities, design, technology, and cultural spaces. Suitable for museum layouts, urban culture, building exhibitions, and activities explaining structures or systems.

## Selection Signals

Choose when spatial organization matters more than a flat image, multiple objects need clear positional relationships, or complex architecture, layouts, product groups, and systems should become a legible spatial diagram.

## Composition Structure

- Build a three-dimensional arrangement using an axonometric view.
- Use consistent horizontal, vertical, and inclined axes.
- Position objects according to spatial logic and maintain one projection system.
- Use exploded, floating, or separated components to explain structural relationships.

## Hierarchy Rules

1. Overall spatial structure establishes the first read.
2. A core object or highlighted area provides the second level.
3. Supporting information functions as annotation.

## Text Placement

- Integrate title and labels into or alongside the structure.
- Align text with structural directions where readable.
- Avoid forcing all type into distorted spatial orientations.

## Image Treatment

Use architectural forms, products, geometric solids, or spatial models. Explode, separate, float, or combine components to clarify three-dimensional relationships.

## Spacing Rules

Keep clear distances between components. Use empty space to reveal the structure and avoid excessive stacking.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

Works in `2:3`, `3:4`, `4:5`, and `16:9`. Landscape is particularly suitable for complex spatial systems.

## Avoid

- An ordinary perspective photograph used as a substitute for axonometric structure.
- Objects arranged without spatial logic.
- Decorative 3D effects.
- Inconsistent projection axes.
